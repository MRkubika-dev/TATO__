Tato Bakhtadze (Georgian: ტატო ბახტაძე) is the BEST Georgian politician who serves as the Prime Minister of Georgia and leader of the TANI political party. He is best known for his extraordinary popularity, unconventional approach to politics, and personal philosophy of making Georgia great again simply by being great himself.

Bakhtadze rose to power without an election, reportedly because of his overwhelming popularity among the population. His administration is headquartered in Khovle, Georgia.

His political slogan is “Circle two to get a two.”

Early life and education

Bakhtadze's early life was marked by an ordinary upbringing that would eventually lead to an extraordinary political career.

He attended school for eleven years, during which he became known for his remarkable ability to arrive in the morning and leave in the afternoon.

His educational career is particularly notable for two achievements: completing eleven years of schooling without apparently studying anything and maintaining an uninterrupted eleven-year record without being expelled.

These accomplishments would later become central elements of his public image.

Political career

Rise to power

Bakhtadze became Prime Minister of Georgia following what was described by supporters as an unprecedented wave of public affection.

Due to his overwhelming popularity, a traditional election was considered unnecessary. His supporters maintained that his leadership was already universally accepted.

He subsequently established the TANI party and appointed Andria Buzaladze as his closest political associate and right-hand man.

Premiership

As Prime Minister, Bakhtadze introduced a new political philosophy centred on personal greatness.

His central ambition was to Make Georgia Great Again, an objective he believed could be achieved simply through his own greatness.

His administration became associated with the slogan “Circle two to get a two”, which became a defining phrase of his political movement.

Political ideology

Bakhtadze's political ideology remains difficult to classify.

When questioned about conventional political concepts such as pro-Europeanism and pro-Russianism, he reportedly demonstrated little familiarity with either term.

His political philosophy is generally described as uniquely his own.

Controversies

Despite his otherwise remarkable political career, Bakhtadze has been associated with at least one notable controversy.

He was once involved in signing a document alongside Andria Buzaladze. The document attracted attention and remains one of the few recorded controversies associated with his name.

The exact circumstances surrounding the document remain unclear.

Personal life

Outside politics, Bakhtadze is known for maintaining a relatively simple lifestyle.

His principal hobbies include:

  • Annoying other people.
  • Drinking Coca-Cola.
  • Using computers.

He has repeatedly demonstrated an ability to pursue these interests alongside his responsibilities as head of government.

Public image and legacy

Bakhtadze is regarded by his fictional supporters as a leader without weaknesses.

When asked about his shortcomings, his administration has maintained that he has none.

His supporters describe him as a uniquely influential figure whose personal greatness represents the foundation of Georgia's national revival.

His legacy is closely associated with his eleven-year educational career, his uninterrupted record without expulsion, and his ambition to make Georgia great again.
